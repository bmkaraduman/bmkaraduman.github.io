Halal Guide – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026 · Developer: Metin Karaduman

Halal Guide is built to process as little personal data as possible. There is no account and no user profile on our servers. Below is what data goes where, and why.

Location

Used for prayer times, the qibla direction and the nearby mosque/restaurant list. Prayer times and qibla are computed on your device; your location is not sent anywhere for them. For nearby places, your location is sent to the Google Places API only at the moment of that search and is not stored by us.

Photos (product scanning)

A product photo you take is sent to the OpenAI API to read and assess the ingredients. The photo stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. Scan history is kept only on your device and can be cleared at any time.

Contacts

Choosing a person for a Friday card uses the iOS system picker; the app does not request access to your contacts and only uses the single name you pick to render that card. No contact data is stored.

Friday message generation

For "Write me a message", the selected language and (if any) the name you chose are sent to the OpenAI API. No other personal data is transmitted.

Notifications

The daily verse/hadith notification is scheduled locally on the device; there are no server push notifications.

Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may process device identifiers and usage data to serve ads and prevent fraud. Users in Europe are shown a consent form; without consent you see non-personalised ads. You may decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt; the app works the same. Details: Google Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

The Pro subscription is purchased through Apple. Your payment details never reach us; Apple only tells the app whether a subscription is active.

Third-party services

  • OpenAI API – product photo analysis and message generation
  • Google Places API – nearby mosques and restaurants
  • Google AdMob – advertising (free version)
  • Apple StoreKit – subscriptions

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights and contact

For any question about your data: bmkaraduman@gmail.com. All on-device data is removed by deleting the app.
