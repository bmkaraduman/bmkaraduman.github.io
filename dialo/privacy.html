This policy explains what data the Dialo Android app processes, why, who receives it, and what you can do about it. Dialo is a language-learning app in which you speak with an AI partner, describe pictures, and follow a learning path from A1 to C2 with words, grammar, exercises and level tests.

In short: What you say is turned into text by your device's speech service. That text goes through our server (Google Firebase in the EU) to OpenAI, which generates replies and corrections; we do not keep a copy of your conversations. Your learning-path progress is stored on our server under an anonymous account so it is not lost. Free users see ads from Google AdMob; Premium subscribers see none. We do not sell data.

1. What is processed

1.1 Your speech

When you tap the microphone, Dialo uses your device's speech recognition service (on most Android devices this is provided by Google) to turn your speech into text. The audio is handled by that service under its provider's terms. Dialo itself does not record, store, or upload audio files; it only receives the resulting text.

1.2 Conversation text, answers and exercise pictures

To generate replies, corrections and feedback, Dialo sends the following to our server, which forwards it to OpenAI's API: the text of what you said or typed, the conversation so far, your selected learning and explanation languages, and — in the picture exercise — the exercise picture supplied by the app. Learning-path answers that need AI grading are handled the same way. Dialo does not access your camera or your photo library and never sends your own photos.

To read texts aloud, the text is sent the same way to OpenAI's text-to-speech service and the returned audio is played on your device. Our server does not store conversation texts or answers; it keeps only a daily usage counter per account to enforce fair-use limits.

1.3 Anonymous account and learning-path progress

So that your learning-path progress (completed units, review items, level results, selected focus) can be saved and restored, Dialo creates an anonymous account with Firebase Authentication when you first use the app. It consists of a random user identifier; it contains no name or email address. Your progress is stored under this identifier in Google Cloud Firestore (EU data centres). Shared learning content (word lists, exercises, audio) is generated once on our server and is the same for all users; it contains no personal data.

1.4 Account data (only if you sign in)

Signing in is optional. If you sign in (email and password, Google, Facebook, or Apple), Firebase Authentication stores your email address, display name if provided, a user identifier, and the sign-in provider, and your progress is moved from the anonymous account to your account. Your password is processed by Firebase and is never visible to us. For security, Firebase also processes IP addresses and device information.

1.5 App integrity checks

To protect our server from abuse, requests are verified with Firebase App Check using Google's Play Integrity API, which confirms that requests come from a genuine copy of the app on a genuine device. This processes device and app integrity information; it is not used for advertising.

1.6 Advertising (free version only)

If you do not have a Premium subscription, Dialo shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's advertising ID, app set ID, IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), information about your interactions with ads and the app (for example app launches and taps), and diagnostic information, and shares them with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. From app version 3.0.1, where the law requires it — in particular in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland — Dialo asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Profile → Privacy settings. Without consent only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Premium subscribers see no ads. See how Google uses data from apps that show ads.

1.7 Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. We receive only the purchase status needed to unlock Premium features. We never receive your payment card details.

1.8 Data stored on your device

Your picture and conversation practice history and results, completed scenarios, word decks, language settings, and subscription status are stored in the app's private storage on your device. PDF reports are created on your device and are shared only when you choose to share them.

Android backup: if you have enabled device backup in your Android settings, Android may include this app data in the backup of your own Google account. That backup is controlled by you and Google; we have no access to it.

1.9 What we do not collect

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.
  • No precise location, contacts, camera, or photo library access.
  • The Facebook SDK is included only to offer "Continue with Facebook". Its automatic event logging and advertising-ID collection are switched off. Data is exchanged with Meta only if you actively choose Facebook sign-in.

3. Who receives data

  • Google (Ireland/USA) — Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage and App Check; processing in EU data centres), the Play Integrity API, Google Play billing, Google Sign-In if you choose it, Google AdMob in the free version, and, on most devices, the speech recognition service. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • OpenAI (USA) — receives, via our server, the texts and exercise pictures described in 1.2. According to OpenAI's published API data policies, content sent through its API is not used to train its models by default and may be retained for a limited period (up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring. See openai.com/policies.
  • Meta — only if you choose Facebook sign-in. Apple — only if you choose Sign in with Apple.

4. International transfers

Some of these providers process data in the United States. Such transfers are based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

5. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Account and learning-path progress (Firebase): until you delete your account. Anonymous accounts that are no longer used remain until you clear the app's data or ask us to delete them.
  • Daily usage counter: contains only the number of AI requests of the current day and is overwritten every day.
  • OpenAI: as described in section 3; we keep no copy.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own retention rules.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play under its own rules; invoicing records we must keep by law are retained as required by German commercial and tax law.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your Dialo account from Firebase Authentication together with your learning-path progress on our server. To remove data stored on your device, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Details and an email option: Delete account.

7. Permissions

  • Microphone — required to recognise your speech during exercises.
  • Internet — required to reach the services above.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob in the free version (see 1.6).

You can change permissions at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Dialo → Permissions.

8. Children

Dialo is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data of children under that age. Users who have not yet reached the age of digital consent in their country (16 in Germany; between 13 and 16 elsewhere in the EU) may only create an account, consent to personalised ads or subscribe with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has created an account without such consent, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Write to smetin.karaduman@gmail.com; we respond within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; for us this is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen.

10. Security

All connections to the services above use TLS encryption. Access to our server requires a valid Firebase account and App Check token. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.
