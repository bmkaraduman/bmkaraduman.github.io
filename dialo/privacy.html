This policy explains what data the Dialo Android app processes, why, who receives it, and what you can do about it. Dialo is a language-learning app in which you speak with an AI partner, describe pictures, and take a practice test.

Data controller

In short: We do not run our own servers and we do not keep a database of your conversations. What you say is turned into text on your device's speech service, that text is sent to OpenAI to generate a reply and corrections, and your practice history stays in the app's storage on your device. We show no ads and we do not sell data.

1. What is processed

1.1 Your speech

When you tap the microphone, Dialo uses your device's speech recognition service (on most Android devices this is provided by Google) to turn your speech into text. The audio is handled by that service under its provider's terms. Dialo itself does not record, store, or upload audio files; it only receives the resulting text.

1.2 Conversation text and exercise pictures

To generate replies, corrections and feedback, Dialo sends the following to OpenAI's API: the text of what you said, the conversation so far, your selected learning and translation languages, and — in the picture exercise — the exercise picture supplied by the app. Dialo does not access your camera or your photo library, and never sends your own photos.

To read replies aloud, the reply text is sent to OpenAI's text-to-speech API and the returned audio is played on your device.

1.3 Account data (only if you sign in)

You can use Dialo without an account. If you choose to sign in (email and password, Google, Facebook, or Apple), authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). It stores your email address, display name if provided, a user identifier, and the sign-in provider. Your password is processed by Firebase and is never visible to us. For security, Firebase also processes IP addresses and device information.

1.4 Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. We receive only the purchase status needed to unlock premium features. We never receive your payment card details.

1.5 Data stored in the app on your device

Your practice history and results, completed scenarios, language settings, and subscription status are stored in the app's private storage on your device. PDF reports are created on your device and are shared only when you choose to share them.

Android backup: if you have enabled device backup in your Android settings, Android may include this app data in the backup of your own Google account. That backup is controlled by you and Google; we have no access to it.

1.6 What we do not collect

  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no ad networks.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • No location, contacts, camera, or photo library access.
  • The Facebook SDK is included only to offer "Continue with Facebook". Its automatic event logging and advertising-ID collection are switched off. Data is exchanged with Meta only if you actively choose Facebook sign-in.

3. Who receives data

  • OpenAI (USA) — receives the text and exercise pictures described in 1.2, and technical connection data such as your IP address. According to OpenAI's published API data policies, content sent through its API is not used to train its models by default and may be retained for a limited period (up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring. See openai.com/policies.
  • Google (Ireland/USA) — Firebase Authentication (if you sign in), Google Play billing, Google Sign-In if you choose it, and, on most devices, the speech recognition service. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Meta — only if you choose Facebook sign-in. Apple — only if you choose Sign in with Apple.

4. International transfers

Some of these providers process data in the United States. Such transfers are based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

5. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Account (Firebase): until you delete your account.
  • OpenAI: as described in section 3; we keep no copy.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play under its own rules; invoicing records we must keep by law are retained as required by German commercial and tax law.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your Dialo account from Firebase Authentication. To remove data stored on your device, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Details and an email option: Delete account.

7. Permissions

  • Microphone — required to recognise your speech during exercises. Asked the first time you record.
  • Internet — required to reach the services above.

You can change permissions at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Dialo → Permissions.

8. Children

Dialo is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data of children under that age. Users who have not yet reached the age of digital consent in their country (16 in Germany; between 13 and 16 elsewhere in the EU) may only create an account or subscribe with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has created an account without such consent, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Write to smetin.karaduman@gmail.com; we respond within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; for us this is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen.

10. Security

All connections to the services above use TLS encryption. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and show a notice in the app.
