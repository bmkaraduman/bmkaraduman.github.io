1. DATA WE COLLECT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Account information

You need an account to use the app. Depending on the sign-in method you
choose, we receive:

  - Email and password
    Your email address. Your password goes to Firebase Authentication in
    encrypted form, never to us; we can never see it.

  - Sign in with Apple
    The name and email Apple passes along. If you use Apple's "Hide My
    Email" feature, we only receive the relay address.

  - Sign in with Google
    Your name and email address.

  - Sign in with Facebook
    Your name and, if you permit it, your email address.

1.2 Profile information

Information you enter inside the app:

  - Name - so we can address you
  - Date of birth - to calculate your zodiac sign and age (required)
  - Gender - to adapt the tone of the texts (optional, may be left blank)
  - Occupation - to make career readings more concrete (optional)

If you leave the gender field blank, we may infer it from your name (see
section 3.2). You can change or reject that guess at any time.

1.2 Profile information (continued)

- Relationship status - to set the tone of love-related readings (optional)
- Current focus - to decide what the daily reading leans on (optional)
- Location - as a CITY AND COUNTRY NAME ONLY, to get the season and the rhythm
  of where you live right (optional)

IMPORTANT NOTE ABOUT LOCATION: If you choose to use your device location, the
coordinates are turned into a city name ON YOUR PHONE ("Istanbul, Türkiye") and
only that text is stored. Latitude and longitude are never sent anywhere -
not to us, not to the AI provider. You can also type your city by hand without
granting location access.


1.3 In-app content

  - Readings you save - which cards were drawn and when
  - Your mood journal - the mood and energy level you pick, and your notes
  - Questions you ask in readings - only when you actually ask one

IMPORTANT: Your journal notes and saved readings are stored ON YOUR DEVICE
ONLY. They are not sent to our servers. If you switch phones, this data does
not travel with you.

1.4 Voice and speech recognition

If you tap the microphone on the "Ask" screen, the question you speak is turned
into text.

- Recording starts ONLY when you tap the microphone and stops on its own when
  you finish speaking.
- The transcription happens ON YOUR DEVICE when a model for that language is
  available; otherwise the audio is sent to Apple's speech recognition service
  (see apple.com/legal/privacy).
- THE AUDIO IS NOT STORED - neither on the device nor on any server. Only the
  resulting text is processed, and only when you submit it as a question.
- You do not have to grant microphone access; you can type your question.


1.5 Advertising and automatically collected data

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. PREMIUM SUBSCRIBERS SEE NO ADS AT ALL -
the moment a subscription is active the advertising layer shuts down completely
and no request reaches the ad network.

While ads are active, AdMob may access:

- The advertising identifier (IDFA) - only if you allow it
- Device and connection data - device model, operating system, IP address,
  approximate (city-level) location
- Ad interactions - which ad was shown, seen, or tapped

APP TRACKING TRANSPARENCY: iOS asks separately for permission to track you
across other companies' apps. You do NOT have to grant it - without it the app
works exactly the same, the ads are simply not personalised. You can withdraw
it any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

IF YOU ARE IN EUROPE: Google's consent form (UMP) is shown for advertising
cookies and personalisation. You can change your choice from inside the app.

AdMob data practices: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Beyond this the app contains no analytics SDK; we do not collect your contacts,
photos, or health data.

Firebase may keep technical logs (IP address, error records, request
timestamps) required to operate the service.

3. SHARING WITH THIRD PARTIES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

We DO NOT SELL your data. Apart from the providers listed below we
share it only with the providers required to run the app.

3.1 Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC)

Used for authentication (Firebase Authentication) and for storing profile
data (Cloud Firestore). Stored data: your account information, name, date of
birth, zodiac sign, gender, occupation, and the cache of your daily content.

Your data may be hosted on Google's servers in the European Union and/or the
United States.
Details: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3.2 OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C. - United States)

PLEASE READ THIS SECTION CAREFULLY. The horoscope readings, card
interpretations, affirmations, and reading syntheses in the app are generated
by artificial intelligence. To do that, the following is sent to OpenAI:

  - Only the FIRST PART of your name (your surname is not sent)
  - Your zodiac sign and your age
  - Your gender and occupation, if you provided them
  - The TEXT OF THE QUESTION you ask in a reading or in the Yes/No section
  - The cards drawn that day and the current moon phase

NOT SENT: your email address, your surname, your mood journal notes, your
saved readings, your full date of birth (only your calculated age is sent).

For the gender inference feature, your name is additionally sent to OpenAI.

OpenAI states that it does not use data submitted through its API to train
its models; it may retain it for a limited period for abuse monitoring.
Details: https://openai.com/policies

Text generated by artificial intelligence is FOR ENTERTAINMENT AND PERSONAL
INSIGHT ONLY. It does not constitute medical, legal, financial, or
psychological advice.

3.3 Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC)

Used to show ads in the free version. Shared: advertising identifier (if you
allowed it), device and connection data, ad interactions. YOUR NAME, BIRTH DATE,
THE QUESTIONS YOU ASK AND YOUR READINGS ARE NEVER SHARED WITH ADMOB. For premium
subscribers AdMob is never initialised at all.

Details: policies.google.com/technologies/ads


3.4 Apple (speech recognition)

When you ask by voice and your device has no on-device model for that language,
the audio is sent to Apple's speech recognition service. Apple processes it
under its own privacy policy. We do not store the audio.

Details: apple.com/legal/privacy


3.5 Sign-in providers

When you sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook, that provider knows the
sign-in took place. Your behaviour inside the app is not shared with them.

  - Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  - Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  - Facebook (Meta): https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

5. RETENTION PERIODS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Account and profile data: for as long as your account is open
  - Daily content cache: from the date generated until your account is deleted
  - On-device data (journal, saved readings): until you delete the app or
    close your account
  - Technical logs: no longer than 12 months

When you delete your account, all of the above is permanently erased.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

You can delete your account at any time, from inside the app, without
contacting anyone:

    Profile -> Account -> Delete my account

For security, you will be asked to re-authenticate and to type DELETE in the
confirmation box. Once confirmed, the following are irreversibly erased:

  - Your Firebase Authentication account
  - Your profile and daily content history in Firestore
  - Your saved readings and mood journal on your device
  - All settings and usage counters on your device

If you cannot open the app, simply write to smetin.karaduman@gmail.com and we
will complete your request within 30 days at the latest.

7. YOUR RIGHTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Under the GDPR and Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK, art. 11) you have the right
to:

  - Learn whether your data is being processed and access it
  - Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  - Request erasure of your data
  - Object to processing and request your data in a portable format
  - Withdraw consent you have given (for example, notifications) at any time

To exercise these rights, write to smetin.karaduman@gmail.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection
Authority (KVKK), or with the data protection authority of your country of
residence in the European Union.

8. SECURITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

All network traffic is encrypted with TLS. Firestore access is restricted by
rules under which each user can reach only their own data. Passwords are
managed by Firebase Authentication; we neither see nor store your password.

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify you
and the competent authorities within the period required by applicable law.

9. CHILDREN
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Semavi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
data from them. In the European Union this age limit may be as high as 16
depending on your country. If you discover that your child has created an
account without permission, write to us and we will delete it.
