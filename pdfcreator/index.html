PDF Scanner & Editor scans documents, turns photos into PDFs and lets you sign, fill in, merge, compress and password-protect PDF files — right on your iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my PDFs stored?

On your iPhone, in the app. You can also find them in the Files app under On My iPhone → PDF Edit and Creator.

How do I sign a PDF?

Open the PDF, tap Sign, draw your signature once, then drag it into place and tap Place. Saved signatures can be reused.

Can I search text in scanned documents?

Yes. With Settings → Recognize text (OCR) turned on, scanned PDFs get a searchable text layer. Use More → Extract text to copy all text.

I forgot the password of a protected PDF.

The password is not stored anywhere, so it cannot be recovered. Keep an unprotected copy if you might need it.

How do I change my ad consent?

Go to Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown in regions where consent is required).
